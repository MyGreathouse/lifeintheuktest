About this site

YourUKHelpDesk’s Life in the UK practice site is a free revision site for people preparing for the Life in the UK Test, the requirement for most applications for settlement and British citizenship. It offers original practice questions, timed mock tests, written study lessons covering all the test chapters, and a tracker that shows your weak areas.

There is no sign-up and no account. Your progress is saved only in your own browser. The site is supported by advertising, which keeps everything free.

Who runs it

The site is built and maintained by Risten Global Ltd, a UK-registered company that creates practical digital tools for people navigating life in the UK. All questions and lessons are written by us in our own words, based on the official handbook. You can read more about how we write and check content on our Editorial Policy page.

Independent resource: this site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Home Office or UK Government. For the official handbook and to book your test, use GOV.UK. Always check current requirements there before applying.
