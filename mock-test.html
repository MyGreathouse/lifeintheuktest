Sample questions

Which Anglo-Saxon king is famous for defending his kingdom against the Vikings?

Correct answer: B) Alfred the Great

Alfred the Great, king of Wessex, successfully resisted the Viking invasions and is the only English monarch called 'the Great'.

The UK is best described as a:

Correct answer: B) Parliamentary democracy with a constitutional monarch

The UK is a parliamentary democracy with a constitutional monarch as head of state.

Which musical genre is strongly associated with British bands of the 1960s such as The Beatles?

Correct answer: A) Pop and rock music

The 1960s saw major British contributions to pop and rock music, led by bands such as The Beatles.

The phrase 'no one is above the law' means:

Correct answer: B) Government ministers and police must also obey the law

It means everyone, whatever their position or wealth, is subject to the same laws.
