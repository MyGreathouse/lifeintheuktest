Life in the UK Test Guide

A Strategic Study System to Pass With Confidence. A companion book to this free practice site, by Jeremy Grant.

Your UK journey starts here

Life in the UK Test Guide is a revision book for anyone preparing for the Life in the UK test, which most people need for British citizenship or settlement (Indefinite Leave to Remain).

It follows the content of the official handbook, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents, regrouped into 12 clear chapters. Each chapter ends with a summary and a short Quick Check, so you can test yourself as you go.

What is inside

  • 12 clear chapters that follow the official handbook, each ending with a summary and a short Quick Check.
  • Topic-by-topic practice questions: 179 in the print edition and 307 in the ebook.
  • Full timed mock exams: 2 in the print edition and 5 in the ebook.
  • An answer key and exam-day tips.

The mock exams use the real test format: 24 questions in 45 minutes, with an 18/24 (75%) pass mark.

Who it is for

People preparing for the Life in the UK Test as part of an application for British citizenship or settlement, including those who want a structured plan on top of the free notes and practice tests on this site.

How it fits with this site

This website stays free: you can take the mock tests, read the chapter study notes and try the full question bank without signing up. The book is an optional extra if you prefer to study from a book, offline, on paper or on a Kindle.
