Life in the UK Test Study Guide

The Life in the UK Test is organised around five official syllabus areas. Our free study mode splits the two largest — history and government — into two parts each, so you can revise seven focused chapters instead of trying to absorb everything at once.

Browse study chapters

Each chapter below links to an untimed study session: answer a question, see the correct answer immediately, and read the explanation before moving on. It's built for learning, not exam pressure.

The Values and Principles of the UK

Democracy, the rule of law, individual liberty, tolerance and mutual respect — the core British values covered in the first section of the test.

What is the UK?

The nations, geography, capital cities, symbols and constitutional make-up of the United Kingdom.

Early Britain to the Stuarts

Prehistoric Britain, the Romans, the Anglo-Saxons, the Normans, the Tudors, and the Civil War through to the Glorious Revolution of 1688.

The Empire to Modern Britain

The Industrial Revolution, the abolition of slavery, the World Wars, the founding of the NHS, and Britain from 1945 to today.

A Modern, Thriving Society

Religion, national festivals, sport, the arts, and everyday customs and traditions across the UK.

Government and Democracy

Parliament, elections, the monarchy, devolution and how the UK is governed.

The Law and Your Role

Courts, the police, tax, local government, and the rights and responsibilities of everyone living in the UK.
