Life in the UK Test: Questions and Answers

Each answer includes a short explanation — not just the correct letter — because understanding why an answer is correct is what actually sticks for exam day.

Sample questions

Which of the following is true of the Life in the UK Test?

Correct answer: B) It is part of the requirement for settlement and citizenship

Passing the Life in the UK Test is normally required when applying for indefinite leave to remain or British citizenship.

Which UK city is known for its university and famous punting on the river?

Correct answer: A) Cambridge

Cambridge is well known for its historic university and for punting on the River Cam.

Stonehenge, in Wiltshire, dates from which period?

Correct answer: A) The Bronze Age

Stonehenge was built in the Bronze Age, though work on the site began earlier in the Stone Age.

Which British engineer designed bridges, railways and ships including the Great Western Railway?

Correct answer: B) Isambard Kingdom Brunel

Isambard Kingdom Brunel was a celebrated Victorian engineer responsible for the Great Western Railway and much else.

Eid al-Fitr marks the end of:

Correct answer: B) Ramadan

Eid al-Fitr is celebrated by Muslims to mark the end of the fasting month of Ramadan.

Pressure groups and charities in the UK:

Correct answer: B) May campaign to influence government policy

Pressure groups, charities and trade unions may lawfully campaign to influence public policy.

Council services are funded partly by:

Correct answer: A) Council tax

Council tax, paid by households, along with central government funding, pays for local services.
